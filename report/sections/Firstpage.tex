\begin{abstract}
This report audits an independent MATLAB reproduction of all displayed computational cases in Sussillo and Abbott (2009) \cite{sussillo_generating_2009}. The implementation covers the Figure 2 output family, Figures 3--5 diagnostics, the three feedback/internal-learning cases in Figure 6, the multifunction and memory tasks in Figure 7, the motion-capture task in Figure 8, and Supplementary Figures S1--S2. Each case uses the architecture shown in the paper; Figure 2D is additionally benchmarked with the Figure 1A, 1B, and 1C architectures. Exact numerical targets are distinguished from structural reconstructions when coefficients or waveforms were not published. At the available scaled network sizes, Figure 2D (1A and 1C), the square wave, internal learning in Figure 6C, four-bit memory, motion capture, PCA diagnostics, gain dependence, and the supplementary spectral effect are reproduced. Failed or partial scaled cases are retained and reported rather than discarded. Code, fixed seeds, tests, figures, CSV summaries, and intermediate MAT files are versioned together.
\end{abstract}
